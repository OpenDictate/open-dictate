# macOS application

Mode: Operate. Native SwiftUI settings and AppKit menu bar utility for macOS 14+.
Inherit OpenDictate's black, graphite, white microphone identity. Use native
controls, system typography, keyboard navigation and English/Russian copy.
The user's instruction delegates technology and implementation choices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Dictate into the app already in use through a global keyboard shortcut.

OWN-WORLD: Black settings canvas, graphite sidebar, white microphone, SF Symbols
and native controls; no marketing imagery or decorative motion.

STORY: Save a key, grant two permissions, choose Live or Accurate, dictate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 780 × 600 settings window; sidebar at left, setup and shortcuts
on the right, mode control below. A menu bar microphone reports recording state.
Recording never takes keyboard focus; Escape cancels only during a session.

FORM: Direct native adaptation of the established Android settings surface;
code-led, no new visual world or composition tournament.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
